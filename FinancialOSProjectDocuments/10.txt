# Entity 001

## FinancialEvidence

### Purpose

FinancialEvidence represents immutable proof that a financial event occurred.

It is the root of trust within FinancialOS.

Every financial record, observation, recommendation, and insight must ultimately trace back to one or more pieces of FinancialEvidence.

Without evidence, FinancialOS should never claim something is true.

---

### Responsibilities

Own the original imported information exactly as it was received.

Preserve provenance.

Track source information.

Allow multiple interpretations without changing itself.

Provide the foundation for explainability.

---

### Does NOT Own

Categories.

Merchants.

Budgets.

Insights.

Recommendations.

User decisions.

Those belong to higher layers.

---

### Invariants

Evidence is immutable.

Evidence always has a source.

Evidence always has an acquisition timestamp.

Evidence may never silently disappear.

Evidence always has provenance.

---

### Relationships

```text

↓

↓
```

### Lifecycle

Created during import.

Never modified.

May receive additional metadata.

May become linked to additional records.

Never deleted under normal circumstances.

May be archived.

# One architectural change I'd make now

There's one thing I would rename before we go any further.

I would replace **FinancialResource** with **Asset** and introduce **Obligation** as its counterpart.

Instead of thinking only in terms of money, the platform can reason about what a user has and what they owe.

* **Asset**: cash, investment accounts, reward points, HSA balances, gift cards, crypto, even PTO if you choose to support it someday.
* **Obligation**: mortgages, credit cards, student loans, taxes due, recurring bills, subscriptions, promised charitable giving.

That gives FinancialOS a richer model of a person's financial world and creates room for future planning and forecasting features without forcing everything into the concept of an account.
